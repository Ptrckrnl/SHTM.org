About the school

We prepare students for leadership roles in the global hospitality and tourism industry. Students learn by doing, from practice in school labs to training in real workplaces.

  • Lab practice

    Kitchen work, food and beverage service, front office, and housekeeping in school training areas.

  • On-the-job training

    Supervised training in hotels, restaurants, and travel and tour companies.

  • Industry partners

    Real work experience with partner businesses, with some international exposure.

  • Events and tours

    Students plan and run school events and tour activities.

Programs

Two four-year degree programs to start your career in hospitality or tourism.

BSHM

4 years

Bachelor of Science in Hospitality Management

Hotel and restaurant operations, front office, housekeeping, food and beverage service, and event management.
